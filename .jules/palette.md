## 2024-06-10 - Improved Screen Reader Support for Timeline Year Tabs
**Learning:** Mapbox popups present a unique challenge for accessibility since they are dynamically injected and destroyed. When creating custom toggle buttons inside a popup that behave like tabs to switch content (e.g., timeline years), relying on `role="group"` and `aria-pressed` can be confusing for screen reader users as it announces them as a group of independent toggle buttons rather than a view switcher.
**Action:** Always upgrade grouped toggle buttons that control distinct views to use the formal `role="tablist"` and `role="tab"` with `aria-selected="true/false"`. This ensures screen readers announce the element contextually (e.g., "Tab 1 of 3") and correctly sets user expectations for how the interaction will behave.
